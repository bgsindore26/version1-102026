Who We Are

MyPersonalCreditScore.com is a simple, independent referral and information service. We help people find trusted credit monitoring, credit building, and credit repair tools in one easy place.

We are not a credit bureau, a lender, a credit repair organization, or a financial advisor. We don't issue credit scores, generate credit reports, or perform any credit repair work ourselves.

What We Do

Our website is a hub that links to carefully selected third-party credit partners. When you click a partner link, you're taken to that partner's own website, where you can explore their services directly.

  • We curate a list of trusted credit-related partners
  • We provide clear, straightforward information
  • We make it easy to compare and reach credit tools
  • We never charge visitors for using our site

What We Don't Do

We do not offer, pay, or guarantee any kind of money, reward, cashback, or financial incentive to visit this website. We are purely a service provider and referral hub. Any offer or incentive you see belongs to a third party, not to us.

  • We don't issue credit scores or credit reports
  • We don't repair credit or provide credit repair services
  • We don't pay visitors to use our site
  • We don't guarantee results from any third-party service

How We Make Money

Our site is free for you to use. We may earn compensation from partner companies when you visit their websites through our referral links. This is a standard referral arrangement — it doesn't cost you anything extra and doesn't affect the information we provide.

Transparency first: We believe in being upfront. If we earn a commission from a partner link, it's because that partner values the referral — not because you're paying us.

Our Commitment to You

We aim to keep things honest, simple, and useful. Our goal is to help you find the right credit tools without confusion or pressure. We encourage you to always review a partner's own terms and privacy policy before signing up for any service.
