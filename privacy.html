Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use MyPersonalCreditScore.com.

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

MyPersonalCreditScore.com ("we," "us," "our") operates as an independent referral service that connects users with trusted credit monitoring, credit building, and credit repair partners. We are not a credit bureau, lender, or credit repair organization. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

By using our site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal financial information to browse our partner listings. However, if you contact us directly by email, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • The content of your message or inquiry
  • Any other information you choose to share

2.2 Automatically collected information

Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information when you visit, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or source
  • General geographic location (country/region level)

This information is used in aggregate to understand how the site is used and to improve the experience.

2.3 Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies to remember preferences and measure site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and email requests
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Understand aggregate site usage and trends
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our site
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Partner Links and Third-Party Sites

Our site contains links to third-party partner websites, including credit bureaus, credit monitoring services, and credit building tools. When you click a partner link and leave our site, you are subject to that partner's own privacy policy and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

Disclosure: MyPersonalCreditScore.com may earn compensation when you visit partner sites through our referral links. This does not affect the price you pay, and it does not influence the information we provide.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who help us operate the site (e.g., hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the site at your own risk.

7. Children's Privacy

Our site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your information
  • The right to opt out of certain data processing activities
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
